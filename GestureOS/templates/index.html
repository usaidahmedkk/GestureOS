About

GestureOS is an AI-powered hand gesture control system that lets you control your computer using just your hands. Built with MediaPipe, OpenCV, and Tone.js, it delivers real-time gesture recognition at 30 FPS with sub-100ms latency.

Draw in the air, play music, control volume and brightness, navigate 3D objects, and give voice commands — all without touching your keyboard.

Python Flask MediaPipe OpenCV Tone.js Voice AI
